# Asks for the owner or Ekky

These are not blocking: everything they unlock is ready behind a switch that stays off.

1. **Hand lettering:**
   - **What:** one sheet in Ekky's hand: Hi!, About, Work, Hire me, Selected work, Thanks, Color, Catch stars, Explore.
   - **Unlocks:** `FEATURES.handLettering`.
   - **Files:** drop them in `assets/hand/`.
2. **A photo or quick self-portrait of Ekky at his desk:** unlocks the About photo slot.
3. **A one-line handwritten note:** "Thanks for stopping by, Ekky".
4. **Client pieces as village scenery:** a yes or no (`FEATURES.clientScenery`).
