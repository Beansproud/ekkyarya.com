About Me

Welcome to my little corner of creativity! I'm Ekky Arya, an illustrator and graphic designer who loves lively, playful work. I give characters a personality you can feel, and I build visuals that make people of every age smile.

From mascots and picture-book scenes to brand identities and social campaigns, I keep the same goal: turn your idea into something warm, memorable and uniquely yours.
